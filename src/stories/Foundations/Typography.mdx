# Typography

The type scale, weights, and font all live in `src/styles/Theme.ts` under `typography` and
`fonts`. Components consume them through the `size`/`weight` props on the `Text` component
(and its presets: `TitleH1`–`TitleH6`, `Paragraph`, `Label`, `Small`, `Caption`) rather than
hardcoding a `font-size` — that's what makes the whole app resize consistently if the scale
ever changes.

Font family: <code>{lightTheme.fonts.lexend}</code>
